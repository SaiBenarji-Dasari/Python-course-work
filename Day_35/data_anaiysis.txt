data analysis is just looking at a bunch of raw infomation & turning it into something USEFUL. like finding the story hiding inside the numbers.

the usual steps 

1.
collect the data (surveys, logs, sales, whatever)

2.
clean it up — fix errors, fill gaps, remove dupes

3.
explore it — look for patterns & weird stuff

4.
analyze — apply stats/math to answer a Question

5.
interpret — what does this actually mean??

6.
share it — charts, reports, dashboards

4 types of analysis

— descriptive: "what happened"

— diagnostic: "why did it happen"

— predictive: "what happens next"

— prescriptive: "what should we DO about it"
